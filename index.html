        Privacy and honesty

        • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking. Your numbers and location stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close it.
        • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs we can't see or control.
        • The prompts in step 1 ask you to paste billing numbers into an AI tool. Never paste card numbers, account IDs or passwords.
        • Volunteer details were read from each organization's own page on the date shown. "Link checked" means the page loaded, not that its content is current.
        • Donation links were found by web search and are only checked for loading.
        • Distances are rough, measured to a central point for each organization or city. Events may be elsewhere.
